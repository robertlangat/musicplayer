# Privacy Policy: Ultra Music Player

**Effective date:** November 17, 2026
**App:** Ultra Music Player (package `com.ultramusicplayer.app`)
**Developer:** Robert Langat
**Contact:** [CONTACT EMAIL]

This policy explains what information Ultra Music Player ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. The app plays music stored on your own device. It has no user accounts and no servers of its own.

## 1. Information that stays on your device

The app uses the following only on your device. **We never receive, upload or share it:**

- **Your music files and their details** (title, artist, album, genre, folder location, artwork, lyric files), read to build your library.
- **Playlists, listening history ("Recently Played"), the play queue and playback position.**
- **Your settings**: equalizer settings, theme, accent color, background skin, shake-to-skip and sorting choices.
- **A background photo**, if you choose one. A copy is stored privately inside the app.
- **Song detail edits.** These are written into your music file when you allow it, or otherwise saved inside the app.
- **Ringtone clips** you create, saved to your device's Music or Ringtones folder.

All of this is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Edited music files and saved clips stay on your device until you delete them.

## 2. Permissions the app requests

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Music and audio access (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`, or storage read on older Android) | Find and play the audio files on your device |
| Folder access via the system folder picker | Include folders you choose, such as on an SD card, and find lyric files in them |
| Notifications | Show playback controls in the notification shade and on the lock screen |
| Foreground service and wake lock | Keep music playing when the screen is off or you use other apps |
| Modify system settings (only if you tap "Set as ringtone") | Set a clip you created as your ringtone |
| Storage write (Android 9 and older only) | Save edited song details and ringtone clips |
| Motion sensor (only if you enable "Shake to skip") | Detect a shake to change track. Sensor readings are not stored or sent anywhere |
| Internet and network state | Load ads, and open music-video searches |
| Advertising ID (`AD_ID`) | Used by Google's ad service, as described below |

You can deny or revoke any permission in your device settings. The related feature then stops working, and the rest of the app continues to work.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

- your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it
- device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version)
- ad interactions (ads shown and tapped) and diagnostic data

This information is collected and processed **by Google**, under Google's own policies. We do not receive information that identifies you personally. Ads in the app are muted so they don't interrupt your music.

- **Consent:** in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Ads & privacy → Privacy options**.
- **Limiting ad tracking:** on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (**Settings → Privacy → Ads** or **Google → Ads**, depending on the device).
- Learn how Google uses this data: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## 4. Music video search (YouTube)

If you use "Search Music Videos", the app opens your search in the YouTube app or your web browser. From that point, YouTube's and Google's terms and privacy policy apply (<https://policies.google.com/privacy>). The app does not download any music or video.

## 5. What we do not do

- We do not require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not collect your music, playlists, listening history or files.
- We do not run our own analytics or tracking servers.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help address it.

## 7. Security

Data handled by the app stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. Data collected by Google for advertising is protected under Google's security practices.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data on our own systems, you can exercise most of these rights directly: clear the app's data, uninstall the app, change your ad consent in the app, or reset your advertising ID. For data processed by Google, see <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. You can also contact us with any request.
